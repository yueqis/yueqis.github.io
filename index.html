Updated Oct 2026

I am a second year PhD student at Carnegie Mellon University, where I am doing research in Natural Language Processing. I am very grateful to be advised by Professor Graham Neubig. Previously, I worked closely with Xiang Yue. I also pursued a BS/MS at CMU, with a dual degree in Computer Science and Statistics and Machine Learning.

I work on agents, especially AI4Science, generalist agentic evaluation and training, and LLM for robotics. I am currently funded by the Meta AIM Fellowship. I am also open to exploring interesting new directions. For more details, please check my CV or hit me up on my email :)
